Mitdenker

A personal record for the Mac.

Mitdenker is a macOS app that keeps one private record per person on their own Mac: mail, calendar, reminders, contacts, notes, calls and job applications in one place, with an assistant that works on that record at the person's request.

Connected accounts

A person may connect their own Google, Microsoft or iCloud account inside the app. The app then reads and changes that account's mail, calendar, tasks and contacts on the person's behalf, from their own Mac. Each connection asks the person for consent on the provider's own sign-in page, and can be disconnected at any time in the app's settings.

How that data is handled is described in the privacy policy.

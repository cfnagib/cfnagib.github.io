Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 September 2026

Where your data lives

Mitdenker keeps your record in a database on your own Mac. There is no Mitdenker server: the app's publisher does not receive, store or see your mail, calendar, tasks, contacts or any other content of your record.

Google account data

When you connect a Google account, Mitdenker asks for these permissions, each only when you turn on the part that uses it:

  • Gmail (gmail.modify) — to show your mail in the app, mark it read or unread, star it, and move it to a label, Spam or Trash at your request. Mitdenker never deletes mail permanently.
  • Google Calendar (calendar) — to show your events and create or change them at your request.
  • Google Tasks (tasks) — to show your tasks and create or change them at your request.
  • Google Contacts (contacts) — to show your contacts and change them at your request.
  • Your email address and basic profile (openid, email, profile) — to name the connected account in the app.

The data is fetched directly from Google to your Mac and kept there. Sign-in tokens are stored only in your Mac's Keychain.

The assistant

If you ask the app's assistant about something in your record, the text needed to answer is sent to the AI provider you have chosen in the app's settings, under that provider's terms, and only when you ask. You can use the app without the assistant.

Sharing

Mitdenker does not sell your data, does not use it for advertising, and does not share it with anyone. It is not used to train AI models by the publisher.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Mitdenker's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

You can disconnect an account in the app's settings at any time, which deletes its sign-in tokens from your Mac. You can also remove Mitdenker's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the app's record on your Mac removes the copied data.
